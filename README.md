### 🚀 Featured Projects

**[SheriaLens — AI & Land Data Project Lead](https://github.com/kacurubisonmeeme)**
AI-powered legal intelligence platform for Kenyan land law research. Engineered an end-to-end pipeline achieving 90% precision in identifying relevant legal precedents, with a chatbot improving legal research efficiency by 85%.

**[Airline Risk Analysis](https://github.com/kacurubisonmeeme/airline-risks-analysis)**
Analyzed 90,000+ FAA/NTSB aviation accident records to identify lowest-risk aircraft for a company entering commercial and private aviation. Extended into a class-balanced Random Forest model and deployed an interactive risk-lookup tool.

**[Student Mental Health Early-Warning Tool](https://github.com/kacurubisonmeeme/University-student-Mental-Health-wellness)**
Predictive screening tool for university counseling centers. Compared Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and Decision Tree classifiers on 27,900+ student records, deploying an interpretable Decision Tree model as a Streamlit app with built-in crisis-resource safeguards.

**GIS & Remote Sensing Coursework**
Digitized spatial features and performed spatial joins in QGIS for a campus mapping project at Dedan Kimathi University; processed Sentinel/Landsat imagery in Google Earth Engine for land cover, flood, and wildfire risk mapping across Kenya; processed drone-captured imagery from RCMRD.
